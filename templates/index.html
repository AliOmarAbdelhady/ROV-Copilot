Rules Applied

  • Platform threat: red if under 5 nm, yellow if 5 to 10 nm, green if over 10 nm.
  • Platform threat becomes green if the iceberg would ground before arrival.
  • Subsea threat is only considered inside a 25 nm subsea zone.
  • Inside 25 nm: red at 90% to 110% of local depth, yellow at 70% to 90%, green otherwise.

Platform depths and coordinates are taken from the PDF table you provided.
